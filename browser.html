Seed corpus: this prototype is loaded with a small real subset of the Aerine ergastorion — erga extracted from published sources under the Collector's procedure, each carrying its verbatim source passage, DOI, and provenance. It is a seed slice, not the full corpus; many gut-health topics are not yet covered, and Aerine will say so plainly when a question outruns what she can trace.   ·   Aerine helps you research; she does not diagnose. Nothing here is medical, clinical, or nutritional advice. Confidence tiers and the golden/loose/broken verdict describe the strength of the evidence chain, not a recommendation to act.
